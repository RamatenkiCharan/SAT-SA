1. Executive Summary & Core Purpose

SAT-SA is an air-gapped supervisory intelligence engine designed to assess the actual operational performance of Security Operations Centers (SOCs) defending Critical Information Infrastructure (CII).


While standard SOC tools (SIEM/SOAR/EDR) respond to threats, SAT-SA audits the defense machinery itself to detect:

  • Execution Gaps (Goodhart's Law Gaming): When documented processes exist and KPIs look healthy (e.g., 99.2% SLA compliance), but underlying evidence shows superficial triage (e.g., 3.2 min triage), missing escalations on critical assets, and recurring un-remediated alerts.
  • Negative Space (Monitoring Blind Spots): Telemetry silence on critical assets that should be producing evidence, gated by Data Trust scores to prevent false alarms during ingestion outages.

3. Analytical Engines & Core Formulas

Data Trust & Quality Score (§7.2.1)

DQ Score = (0.35 × Completeness) + (0.25 × Consistency) + (0.25 × Coverage) + (0.15 × Sample Sufficiency)

Gated mechanism: Negative space silence alerts and high-priority findings are gated when DQ Score < 0.70 to eliminate false alarms during telemetry dropouts.

Peer Benchmarking & Robust Statistics

MAD = median(|Xᵢ - X̃|)   |   Robust Z = 0.6745 × (Xᵢ - X̃) / MAD

Computes peer-relative deviations with minimum cohort size thresholding (cohort ≥ 5) and automatic global fallback.

Evidence Fusion & Priority Scoring (§10.5)

Priority Score = 0.30·Signal + 0.25·PeerDev + 0.20·Persistence + 0.15·Criticality - 0.10·Uncertainty

Contract: Every High/Critical priority finding strictly requires ≥ 2 independent corroborating signals.
